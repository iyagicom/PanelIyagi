# Privacy Policy for PanelIyagi

**Last updated: October 21, 2026**

## Overview
PanelIyagi is a taskbar and panel for Ubuntu GNOME.

## Data Collection
PanelIyagi does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer. Settings and sticky notes stay on your own PC. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

## Network Use
PanelIyagi does not need an internet connection and does not send any data to external servers.
